How to read this

Each point is the last price of a 5-minute window, turned into two exponential moving averages: EMA38 reacts quickly, EMA100 slowly. When the fast line moves above the slow line the price has started to rise (BUY); below, it has started to fall (SELL). The shaded band between the lines is blue while fast is above slow and orange while it is below. The time axis skips the hours with no trading. All times are local exchange time (CET in November). Equal-start signals: both EMAs start from the first price, so they are exactly equal after window 1. The literal spec rule then fires on the second window even though nothing crossed. These are drawn hollow and are excluded from the heat map; --strict-crossover removes them.
